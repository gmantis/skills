# A positive screening result: how worried should you be?

*Patient leaflet (synthetic eval source).*

A screening test for a condition that **1 in 100** people of your age have. The test catches **90 %** of people who have
the condition (sensitivity) and correctly clears **91 %** of people who don't (specificity).

Picture **1000 people** like you:
- 10 have the condition; the test flags **9** of them.
- 990 don't; the test still flags **89** of them by mistake.

So of the **98** people who test positive, only **9** have the condition: about **9 %**. A positive result means
"more tests", not "you have it". If the condition were as common as 1 in 10, the same test's positive result would be
right about half the time (53 %).
