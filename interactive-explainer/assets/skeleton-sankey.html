How the model works

REPLACE: each source sends its records to Validate, which rejects a share that depends on the source (web forms fail more often). What passes goes to Deduplicate, which removes a share as duplicates; the rest is split between auto-approval and manual review, and review passes most of its records to Loaded and quarantines the rest. Each link carries two measures, records and megabytes (records × average size per source), so the same flow can be read either way.

Conservation is built in: every stage's inflow equals its outflow plus its losses, for both measures, and the validation table checks it live. Removing a stage either reroutes its inflow straight on to its normal outputs in the same ratios (its losses no longer happen) or drops it into a new loss; downstream stages keep their split ratios.
