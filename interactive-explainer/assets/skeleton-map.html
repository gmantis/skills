How the model works

Each hotspot cluster sends smoke in proportion to its hotspot count. The share that reaches the city is cos²(angle) × e−distance / reach: the angle is between the direction the wind blows towards and the great-circle line from the cluster to the city (zero if the wind blows away from the city), and the reach grows with the wind speed (120 km per m/s). The wind is the same everywhere. Country values are the sums over their clusters, so the bars, the colours and the total always add up. illustrative hotspot counts, reach and the transport rule are made up for the skeleton (REPLACE).

Geography: Natural Earth 1:110m countries, Natural Earth I projection. Each cluster is assigned to the country polygon that contains it (point in polygon), and the validation table checks that those totals match the source table. Small states such as Singapore are not polygons at this scale, so the city is drawn as a point.
