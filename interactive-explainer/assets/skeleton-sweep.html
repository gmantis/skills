Does the model reproduce the known values?

Wq=1+cs22·C(c,a)cμ−λ: the wait = (how uneven service is) × (chance of having to wait ÷ spare capacity). Exact for one server (Pollaczek–Khinchine) and for random service (Erlang C); two servers with constant service use it as an approximation.

How the model works

REPLACE: λ customers per minute arrive at random (a Poisson stream) and c servers each take s minutes on average, with variability cs² (0 = every service takes exactly s, 1 = exponential). Utilisation ρ = λ/(c/s). Below ρ = 1 the queue settles to a steady state with the average wait above; at or above 1 it has no steady state and grows by about c(ρ−1) people per service time, so the page says "grows without limit" instead of a number.

The six panels and "one run" are a seeded discrete-event simulation (the same random arrivals for every value), so they differ from the formula by chance; the validation table checks 200 000 customers against it. The knee is a page convention (the first load where +10 points adds more than one service time) illustrative.
