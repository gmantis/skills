Does the model add up?

How the model works

Totals. Only leaves carry numbers (here bytes and files); every folder is the sum of its visible parts, computed bottom-up. Hiding a branch takes it out of every total above it, so all shares re-total. The detail card says what a hidden branch would add back.

Boxes. A squarified treemap (Bruls, Huizing and van Wijk, 2000): each box's area is exactly proportional to its amount, two levels at a time, zoomed to the selected folder. A name is drawn only when it fits; otherwise read it in the tree, the tooltip or the card. Parts far below 1% become slivers or vanish; that is honest, and the tree still lists them.
