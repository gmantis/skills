How the model works

A small event simulator replays the exchange: every message takes the network delay per hop, the worker takes its processing time, and the client's timer fires after the timeout. Each message or action becomes one trace step, with a snapshot of what every actor knows at that moment. The rules are checked at every step; the first step that breaks one is marked in red.

The reference run is the correct protocol (with the key) under the same conditions, so the comparison shows exactly where the what-if goes wrong.
