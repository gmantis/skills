How the model works

REPLACE: requests arrive at random (a Poisson stream, λ per second, seeded so the trace is repeatable). The bucket starts full with b tokens and gains r tokens per second, never more than b. A request that finds at least one token takes it and passes; otherwise it is rejected and takes nothing. Everything on the page (figure, chart, readout) is computed from that one rule at the selected time.

The long-run share is exact: the bucket's shortfall b − tokens behaves like the work in a queue with one server, fixed service time 1/r and room for b requests (M/D/1/K, K = b), whose loss share comes from its embedded Markov chain. As b grows it tends to min(1, r/λ). The figure's motion is eased between selected times; the positions of boxes and the barrier are pure functions of time, so scrubbing backwards works.
