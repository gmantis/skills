How the model works

Each option's expected cost over the years you keep it = price + filters + electricity (hours a night × nights a year × years × watts ÷ 1000 × price per kWh) + expected replacements (years × yearly chance of a breakdown × price), or the rental fee, which includes filters and repairs. Every cost is a straight line in each input, so each break-even point has a closed form, and each tornado bar is exactly the slope times the range. The tree is the same sum written as a decision: for an owned unit, two outcomes (it lasts, or it needs replacing once) weighted by their chances give the expected cost. Expected value is the right yardstick when the stakes are small next to your budget; if one breakdown would really hurt, weigh the worst branch too.
