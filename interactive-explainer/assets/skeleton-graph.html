How the model works

Each box's value is its outside input plus the sum of weight × value over the arrows into it: x = ext + W·x. The page solves it by rounds of influence (each round passes every box's value one arrow further), which is what the replay shows. Because the model is linear, the outcome is exactly the sum of "only driver A on" and "only driver B on", which is the split in the bar chart.

A loop's gain is the product of the weights around it: negative = balancing (B), positive = reinforcing (R). A lone loop with gain of 1 or more, or −1 or less, never settles; the page then says "runs away" instead of quoting numbers.
