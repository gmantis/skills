How the model works

REPLACE: the model is the source's change log as data: each change has a date (year, month or day, printed at that precision), a lane (its kind), a short label, a sentence and a citation; changes that took months have an end date. Eras are the source's own periods; a change belongs to the era its start date falls in, and a span must end inside that era. "Led to" links are the source's own cause-and-effect statements, never inferred from dates alone.

The timeline packs labels into up to three rows per lane: right of the dot, else left of it, else hidden (the dot stays and the card shows it). Density view counts changes per year (per quarter when zoomed under three years).
