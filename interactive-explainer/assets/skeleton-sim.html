How the model works

The room holds N0 = 400 tracer particles; each stands for C0·V / N0 of PM2.5. Every tick (3 s) each particle takes a random step whose spread matches turbulent diffusion with coefficient D (“air mixing”), bouncing off the walls. A particle inside a purifier's intake zone is caught with the chance that would remove CADR/V of the room's air per hour if the particles were spread evenly. An open window removes particles the same way at the air-change rate a, and lets outdoor air in: new particles appear at the window at a·Cout.

The formula is the well-mixed mass balance dC/dt = −(k + a)·C + a·Cout with k = (number of purifiers)·CADR/V, so C(t) = Css + (C0 − Css)·e−(k+a)t and Css = a·Cout/(k + a). The simulation agrees with it only when the air mixes faster than the purifier cleans it: step 4 shows what still air does.
