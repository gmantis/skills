Friendly fire — why sunlight is healthy

Chapter 9 is where the book stops listing molecules that sunlight makes (vitamin D, nitric oxide) and offers a bigger idea. Your immune system is a fire brigade. Modern indoor life leaves it idle, so it smoulders. Sunlight, like exercise, is a short, controlled fire that trains the brigade to put fires out. The page below simulates that idea: pick a life, play 60 days and watch the embers.

“Sun exposure improves the same health conditions as exercise because it is a form of exercise. It’s a workout for the skin.” — Jacobsen, Ch 9

For sun exposure and MS, I would say yes.” She reports the same pattern in pediatric MS, Crohn’s disease and type 1 diabetes. Two arms, two responses

UV mostly calms the adaptive immune system (the targeted arm that learns specific threats and can turn on your own nerves, gut or pancreas). It stimulates the innate arm, the general repair crew that walls off wounds and clears damaged cells. The big guns stand down while local repair goes to work.

Why a “double whammy” became a feature

Kripke showed that UV both damages DNA and switches off the surveillance that kills “baby cancers”. In prehistoric skin, with melanin, repair systems and sun-adapted microbes, that trade was cheap: tolerance instead of “constant inflammation, beset with rashes and hives”. Today’s sun-naive skin pays more for it, and the price rises steeply with a burn.

    4 · Glossary

    Inflammation
    The immune system’s battle response: heat, swelling and chemical weapons, with some unavoidable “friendly fire” on healthy tissue.
    Resolution
    The switch to anti-inflammatory mode afterwards: “turn off the flamethrowers, clean up the debris, heal the damage.”
    Inflammaging
    Chronic, low-grade, smouldering inflammation that grows with age in modern people. It is not seen in hunter-gatherers.
    Hormesis
    A small dose of stress that makes the body stronger: exercise, plant compounds, sauna or cold, fasting, and sunlight.
    Fire drill
    Jacobsen’s image for exercise. It is pro-inflammatory in the short run and anti-inflammatory in the long run, because the repair crew it recruits also puts out unrelated fires.
    Adaptive immunity
    The targeted arm that learns specific pathogens or cancer cells. Autoimmune disease happens when it targets your own cells. UV calms it.
    Innate immunity
    The general damage-control arm: walls off wounds, removes compromised cells, makes repairs. Light stimulates it.
    Immune tolerance
    Learning to hold fire at harmless things (peanuts, farm microbes, blackfly bites, daily sun) instead of overreacting.
    Immune surveillance
    The skin’s patrol that kills newly mutated cells. UV suppresses it locally for a while.
    Urocanic acid (UCA)
    A skin molecule made by sunlight and a main trigger of UV immunosuppression. Sun-loving skin bacteria eat it.
    Narrowband UV (311 nm)
    Clinical phototherapy for psoriasis, vitiligo and in MS trials. It avoids the 290–300 nm wavelengths that do most direct DNA damage.
    Clinically isolated syndrome
    A first MS-like episode that often progresses to full multiple sclerosis. It was the target of Hart’s trial.
    Polymorphic light eruption (PLE)
    An itchy rash after sun in people whose immune systems respond strongly. They get less skin cancer.
    Burn dose (MED)
    The UV dose that just turns your skin pink. The simulator’s sun slider is a share of it; darker skin has a bigger burn dose.

    How the model works

    Hourly simulation over 60 days, after a 150-day warm-up in a sun-less, drill-less version of the same life. Acute inflammation gets pulses from exercise at 07:00, sun at 09:00 and an allergen at 15:00, and decays with a 6-hour half-scale. It recruits a resolution crew that lingers for about 30 hours and lowers inflammaging, which is fed by the irritant load. Sun below the burn dose adds a systemic calming signal (about 4 days) and tolerance training (months), which shrink allergen flares. Sun above the burn dose adds burn damage that feeds inflammaging instead of training anything. UV also causes DNA damage and a local surveillance dip; skin microbes shrink the dip. Damage that meets a dipped surveillance counts as escaped. Exercise above 1.1 adds lasting damage (“running until your arches collapse”). Units are arbitrary.
